MAX TINDALL

Max Tindall is an artist based in Philadelphia, PA. Working primarily in watercolour and oil, they document the dark and often chaotic scenes unfolding around them — drawing particularly on the decay and corruption endemic to their studio in Kensington.

Max graduated in 2021 and has since worked across fine art, software development, blockchain engineering, and machine learning — treating each as a distinct but related practice.
