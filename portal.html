Portal is a verified private communications platform for high-trust professional environments. Where existing video tools verify devices, Portal verifies people — combining peer-to-peer WebRTC video with biometric identity confirmed on the blockchain and tamper-evident session audit records.

There is no server that touches the call. There is no recording. There is no third party with access to the session. Attorney-client privilege is structural, not contractual.

At purchase, the buyer's face is hashed and written permanently on-chain — a one-time, immutable biometric commitment tied to their wallet. When both parties join a session, on-device face recognition confirms each keyholder is physically present. Every session produces a tamper-evident audit record, verifiable against the public Solana ledger. Both parties join a session directly through the browser — no download, no account. On-device face recognition confirms each keyholder is physically present. When verified, both parties see a live confirmation.

Built for legal professionals, financial advisors, and anyone whose communications require verifiable identity and structural confidentiality. Initial deployment targets attorney-client communications — a market where impersonation and privilege are not edge cases but existential concerns.

No download. No account. Works in any browser.
